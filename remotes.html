Remotes

Each Harmony model stores its setup differently, so each one is added on its own and tested on real remotes before it's called supported.

  • In every release and tested on real remotes.
  • Works, but few people have tried it yet. Please report how it goes.
  • In a public test build on the download page, not yet in a release.
  • Being tried privately with volunteers on Discord; not in any download yet.
  • Ready to add as soon as someone with one sends us its configuration on Discord.
  • Not usable with Afterglow yet.

Point at a remote to take a closer look. Click it for the details.

Harmony 900 Supported

2009

Everything you could set up with Logitech's software:

  • Devices and activities, including which device each button controls in an activity
  • Every physical button, and the power plan: what turns on, in what order, and what turns off
  • Input switching and channel entry for each device
  • IR output per device: the remote's own emitter, or the RF blaster and its two mini emitters; new blasters can be paired
  • Remote settings and the icons the remote shows

Harmony 1100 Experimental

2009

Afterglow reads, imports, builds and writes 1100 configurations. Before each write it saves what's on the remote, so you can always put it back.

  • Devices and activities, with every button and the power plan
  • The touchscreen as the 1100 shows it: transport, numbers, favourites with their logos, and your own page of buttons
  • The optional RF extender isn't supported yet, so the 1100 stays experimental until it is.

Harmony One / One+ Supported Beta

2008

The later Harmony One+ is the same remote with newer software inside. Afterglow sets up both the same way, so everything here goes for either.

The One is supported in the beta. Afterglow builds its setup from your devices and activities, or brings in the one already on your remote:

  • Devices and activities with their pages, favourite channels, and the welcome after flashing
  • Its settings: how long the screen stays lit, the 12- or 24-hour clock, the Remote Assistant and the language

Get the beta from the download page, under “Try the next version”, and tell us how it goes on the Discord.

Harmony 800 Experimental Beta

Prototype, never sold

Logitech never sold the 800; only prototypes exist. Afterglow builds its setup from your devices and activities, as it does for the One, and a tester's remote runs it: activities and devices send their commands, and it sleeps and wakes as it should. It's in the beta and comes to a release with the One. So few exist that it stays experimental.

  • The slide-out keyboard, the touchpad and learning from other remotes aren't supported yet.

Get the beta from the download page, under “Try the next version”, and tell us how it goes on the Discord.

Harmony 650 / 665 Experimental Beta

2010

The 665 is the 650's later version. The 660 looks the same as the 665; nobody has tried one with Afterglow yet, so its configuration is wanted. Afterglow sets both up the same way: it reads what's on your remote and builds and writes a new setup. Before each write it saves what's on the remote, so you can always put it back.

  • Devices and activities, with Logitech's own page layouts
  • Choose which activity Watch TV, Watch a Movie and Listen to Music start
  • Favourite channels by name; these remotes show no logos
  • Logitech's welcome tour after the first flash

Get the beta from the download page, under “Try the next version”, and tell us how it goes on the Discord.
